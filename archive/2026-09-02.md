# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-09-02

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desaforado** - frantic; unrestrained; out of control
2. **atenazar** - to grip tightly; to hold in a painful or oppressive grip
3. **desperdigados** - dispersed, isolated, scattered; strewn about
4. **peripecias** - vicissitudes; twists and turns
5. **calabozo** - jail cell; dungeon; lockup
6. **amagar con hacer algo** - to make as if to do something; to start or feint at doing something
7. **azuzar** - to incite; to egg on
8. **desdichado** - unfortunate; wretched; unlucky; miserable (adj)
9. **subastar** - to auction
10. **hurtar** - to steal; to take away; to usurp (more formal than robar, and without violence)
