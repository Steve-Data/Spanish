# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-11-15

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desaforado** - frantic; unrestrained; out of control
2. **con todo** - nevertheless; even so
3. **están embobados** - to be stunned; to be spellbound; to be dumbfounded (not: están embelezados)
4. **no dar pie con bola** - to be unable to get anything right; to be completely at a loss
5. **aterida** - freezing; numb with cold
6. **irrebatible** - irrefutable; impossible to refute or argue against
7. **afanarse** - to strive; to toil; to work hard (not: esmerarse)
8. **mecer** - to rock; to sway
9. **altaneras** - arrogant; haughty; conceited; disdainful
10. **escabroso** - thorny; tricky; lurid; risqué; rough or treacherous
