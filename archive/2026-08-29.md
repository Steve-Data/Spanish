# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-08-29

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **estar despachado** - to be finished; to be done for
2. **azorado** - flustered; bewildered; embarrassed
3. **atenazar** - to grip tightly; to hold in a painful or oppressive grip
4. **canaleta** - gutter; channel; drain
5. **desdeñosa** - disdainful; scornful; contemptuous (adj)
6. **desde luego** - of course; certainly
7. **acérrimo** - staunch; bitter
8. **berreta** - cheap, crappy, low-quality, tacky
9. **entorpecer** - hinder, hamper, impede (not: estorbar)
10. **a la par que** - at the same time as
