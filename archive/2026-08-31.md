# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-08-31

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desaforado** - frantic; unrestrained; out of control
2. **estar despachado** - to be finished; to be done for
3. **atenazar** - to grip tightly; to hold in a painful or oppressive grip
4. **andar a las corridas** - to rush around; to be run off one's feet (Arg)
5. **ociosas** - idle; inactive; unoccupied; pointless
6. **darse pena a sí mismo** - to feel sorry for oneself; to pity oneself
7. **diseminados** - scattered; spread out; dispersed (slightly formal, not the more common desparamados)
8. **berreta** - cheap, crappy, low-quality, tacky
9. **revoloteaban** - to flutter around; to flit
10. **cobija** - blanket (not: manta)
