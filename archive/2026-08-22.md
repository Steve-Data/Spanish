Updated: 2026-08-22

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

2. **desperdigados** - dispersed, isolated, scattered; strewn about
3. **canaleta** - gutter; channel; drain

8. **disparatado** - absurd, nonsensical, wildly unreasonable
9. **acérrimo** - staunch; bitter
10. **acatar una orden** - to obey an order; to comply with an order
