# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-08-16

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desperdigados** - dispersed, isolated, scattered; strewn about
2. **atenazar** - to grip tightly; to hold in a painful or oppressive grip
3. **idónea** - suitable, appropriate, right for a purpose; sometimes “ideal”
4. **en ayunas** - on an empty stomach; fasting
5. **dirimir** - to resolve; to annul
6. **irrebatible** - irrefutable; impossible to refute or argue against
7. **encogidos** - huddled; shrunk; curled up
8. **el resquicio** - crack; small opening; trace; loophole
9. **apresar** - to seize; to capture; to apprehend
10. **causar estragos** - to wreak havoc; to cause devastation
