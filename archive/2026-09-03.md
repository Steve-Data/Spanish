# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-09-03

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desaforado** - frantic; unrestrained; out of control
2. **atenazar** - to grip tightly; to hold in a painful or oppressive grip
3. **desperdigados** - dispersed, isolated, scattered; strewn about
4. **anonadado** - dumbfounded; stunned; speechless
5. **extrañada** - surprised, puzzled, taken aback; not “missed”
6. **el derrotero** - route; course; path
7. **cobija** - blanket (not: manta)
8. **repicar** - to ring; to chime (as in the sound of bells)
9. **reacios** - reluctant; unwilling; resistant
10. **despampanante** - stunning, show-stopping, eye-catching; usually of a person, outfit, or appearance
