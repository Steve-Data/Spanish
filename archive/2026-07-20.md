# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-07-20

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desperdigados** - dispersed, isolated, scattered; strewn about
2. **el resquicio** - crack; small opening; trace; loophole
3. **discurría** - to run or flow along; to unfold; to think through
4. **canaleta** - gutter; channel; drain
5. **ultrajar** - to violate; to outrage; to desecrate
6. **andamiaje** - scaffolding; framework; support structure
7. **apañarse con** - to manage with; to make do with; to get by with
8. **pacata** - prudish, narrow-minded, socially conservative; timid or conventional in attitude
9. **farfullar** - to mumble; to babble; to speak unclearly (not: balbucear)
10. **azorado** - flustered; bewildered; embarrassed
