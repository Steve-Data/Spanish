# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-11-13

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desaforado** - frantic; unrestrained; out of control
2. **con todo** - nevertheless; even so
3. **están embobados** - to be stunned; to be spellbound; to be dumbfounded (not: están embelezados)
4. **sobremanera** - excessively; greatly
5. **regañadientes** - grudgingly; reluctantly
6. **la apuró** - drained it; finished the last of it
7. **un berretín** - a fixation; a whim; a pet ambition (Arg)
8. **increpar** - to confront; to rebuke harshly
9. **verse abocado a** - to be driven or forced into
10. **a destajo** - intensely; without stopping; at top speed
