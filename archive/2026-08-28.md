# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-08-28

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **azorado** - flustered; bewildered; embarrassed
2. **estar despachado** - to be finished; to be done for
3. **atenazar** - to grip tightly; to hold in a painful or oppressive grip
4. **despedazados** - torn to pieces; ripped apart; shredded
5. **amagar con hacer algo** - to make as if to do something; to start or feint at doing something
6. **el barquinazo** - sudden lurch; jolt
7. **dentro de todo** - all things considered; on the whole
8. **repicar** - to ring; to chime (as in the sound of bells)
9. **despiadada** - ruthless, pitiless, cruel; showing no mercy
10. **extraviada** - lost; misplaced; astray (adj)
