# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-07-06

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **amonestación** - warning; admonition; reprimand
2. **despedazados** - torn to pieces; ripped apart; shredded
3. **pérfida** - treacherous, deceitful, malicious, false-hearted
4. **irrebatible** - irrefutable; impossible to refute or argue against
5. **dejarse amilanar** - to let oneself be intimidated; to be cowed
6. **no venir a cuento** - to be irrelevant; to be beside the point
7. **pregunta capciosa** - trick question; leading question
8. **ensartar** - to thread; to string; to skewer (Spa)
9. **arredrarse** - to be intimidated; to lose heart; to back down (literary not conversational)
10. **dar un ramalazo de** - to feel a sudden pang or burst of
