# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-08-15

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desperdigados** - dispersed, isolated, scattered; strewn about
2. **atenazar** - to grip tightly; to hold in a painful or oppressive grip
3. **idónea** - suitable, appropriate, right for a purpose; sometimes “ideal”
4. **calabozo** - jail cell; dungeon; lockup
5. **escabroso** - thorny; tricky; lurid; risqué; rough or treacherous
6. **artero** - treacherous; deceitful
7. **apabullante** - overwhelming; crushing; staggering (not: avasallante)
8. **alejamiento** - distance; estrangement; separation
9. **por lo pronto** - for now; for the time being
10. **arañazos** - scratches
