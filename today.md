Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

2. **desbocado** - out of control; runaway; baggy
3. **la colilla** - cigarette butt
4. **un vericueto** - a twist or turn; a complication; a winding path
5. **extrañada** - surprised, puzzled, taken aback; not “missed”
6. **desdeñosa** - disdainful; scornful; contemptuous (adj)

9. **no estar para hacer algo** - not to be in the mood or condition to do something
10. **afanarse** - to strive; to toil; to work hard (not: esmerarse)
