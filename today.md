# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-11-20

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desaforado** - frantic; unrestrained; out of control
2. **susodicho** - aforementioned
3. **están embobados** - to be stunned; to be spellbound; to be dumbfounded (not: están embelezados)
4. **la soltura** - ease; fluency; confident skill

6. **roñosa** - mangy; filthy; shabby; miserly
7. **hurtar** - to steal; to take away; to usurp (more formal than robar, and without violence)
8. **incidir en** - to affect; to influence; to have an effect on
9. **escultórica** - sculptural; statuesque
10. **extrañada** - surprised, puzzled, taken aback; not “missed”
