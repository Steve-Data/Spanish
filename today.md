# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-06-28

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into questions, mini-dialogues, and correction exercises.

1. **canaleta** - gutter; channel; drain
2. **irrebatible** - irrefutable; impossible to refute or argue against
3. **embaucar** - to deceive; to swindle; to trick; to con
4. **no venir a cuento** - to be irrelevant; to be beside the point
5. **embotado** - dulled, numbed, or mentally foggy; blunted, especially of the mind, senses, or an edge
6. **apresurar** - to hurry; to rush; to hasten
7. **plasmar** - to express; to capture; to give shape to
8. **refunfuñar** - to grumble; to mutter complaints
9. **qué guachada** - What a shame; what a drag; how disappointing
10. **desafilar** - to make a blade or edge dull; to blunt
