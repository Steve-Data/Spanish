# Spanish Reinforcement Words

Updated: 2026-11-17

Use these exact Spanish targets in today's Spanish practice. Work them naturally into exercises.

1. **desaforado** - frantic; unrestrained; out of control
2. **están embobados** - to be stunned; to be spellbound; to be dumbfounded (not: están embelezados)
3. **se apretujó** - squeezed or huddled close
4. **embaucar** - to deceive; to swindle; to trick; to con
5. **hurtar** - to steal; to take away; to usurp (more formal than robar, and without violence)
6. **empañar** - to fog up; to tarnish; to blur
7. **ociosas** - idle; inactive; unoccupied; pointless
8. **renuencia** - reluctance; unwillingness; hesitation (Spa)
9. **entumecidos** - numb; stiff
10. **transigir con** - to compromise with; to give in to; to accept reluctantly
